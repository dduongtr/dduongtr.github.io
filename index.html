Duy Duong‑Tran, Ph.D.

Assistant Director of Graduate Studies, Data Science, Harvard University. Affiliated faculty, Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania.

I lead graduate education and run a research program that connects the brain, mathematics and AI.

My research covers network neuroscience, biomedical language and vision models, and engineering education. From 2023 to 2026 I was a tenure-track faculty member in Mathematics at the U.S. Naval Academy. I hold a Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from Purdue University and worked at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory during my doctorate.

Research themes

Three connected threads, with the brain as the common starting point.

Network neuroscience and Neuro-AI

Topology, geometry and information theory applied to brain networks, and what brain-inspired structure can teach machine learning models.

Biomedical language and vision models

Large language models, knowledge graphs and multimodal learning for Alzheimer’s disease, imaging genetics and clinical data.

Engineering education

How cognitive diversity, motivation and grit relate to academic performance in multidisciplinary engineering classrooms.
